Tap the dark to make a pearl. Touch a pearl to hear its voice. Drag and release to set it in motion. Collisions sing; ripples wake nearby matter. Select a pearl to reveal its own instrument. Drag the small control spheres up or down; arrow keys work too.

Matter moves from struck glass to a breath of choir. Bloom opens the space around each note. Gravity draws the world into orbit. Time changes its pace without changing pitch.

Each pearl has its own volume ADSR, three-band EQ, and three oscillator layers. Give each layer a waveform, octave, level, and detune. New envelopes and waveforms begin with your next touch. Play auditions your changes. Touch Universe to return to the world controls.

Wander sends a gentle impulse through the field. Hold suspends physics while you play. The small circle above records the sound to a downloadable file.
